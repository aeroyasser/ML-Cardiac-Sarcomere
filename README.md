- This project proposes a machine learning (ML) framework based on artificial neural networks (ANNs) as an accurate surrogate model for cardiac sarcomere contraction dynamics. The ANN is trained on synthetic datasets generated from the Rice myofilament model under isosarcometric, cell-shortening, and isometric protocols across physiological ranges. Results show that shallow networks underfit transient twitch dynamics, whereas a deep network significantly improves prediction accuracy, stability, and generalization. The trained model accurately predicts force and shortening dynamics in both rat and rabbit myocardium and successfully captures species-specific mechanical responses. Across all contraction modes, the deep ANN reproduces both the amplitude and temporal features of sarcomere behavior under previously unseen conditions. Despite the complexity of cardiac sarcomere dynamics arising from nonlinear interactions governing calcium activation, cross-bridge cycling, and force generation, the proposed ML framework bridges mechanistic modeling and data-driven learning, enabling rapid and biophysically consistent predictions of cardiac muscle twitch dynamics.
